Our story · How we work

From the land’s history to a better harvest.

01

We study the land's history.

TerraMap reads years of satellite history: past crops, land use and weak zones. We know the field before we touch the soil.

02

Soil tested on the farm.

SoiLenz 2.0 completes the full analysis in 15 minutes: 14 parameters, right there in the village.

03

Instant ticket. AI advisory report.

A printed soil ticket in the farmer's hand at once, then an AI-generated advisory report on soil quality and the best crops to grow.

04

Tickets become nutrient maps.

Every ticket's values go into GeoLenz. It maps N, P, K, pH and organic matter across the whole field, zone by zone.

05

Speak with our team.

Book a slot for a detailed, customised session. Together we plan your crop cycle for the seasons ahead.

06

Soil Amendment Playbook.

A week-by-week plan to rebuild the soil, so every crop grows stronger than the last.
